See an XML file as a solar system

Open or drop any XML file, or paste XML anywhere on this page. The root element is the sun. Each element's children orbit it, each on an orbit of its own. Click a body to centre it and bring the elements it is linked to next to it; click it again to open its own orbits, or show every orbit at once with the button at the bottom right. What orbits what never changes; you can drag bodies about and they drift back. That is the Orbit view. The Tree view, chosen in the top bar, shows the same elements as a tree: the root element at the bottom, and each element's children above it, on branches that curve upward and never lean more than 45° to either side, except the branches stretched to the elements gathered round the selected one. The side panel explains what each element is in plain words, with its definition when the file, or a schema or catalogue you open as a dictionary, defines its name.
